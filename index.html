/ About the workshop

Geometry as a foundation for physical intelligence.

Geometry provides a common structure for understanding robotic bodies, their environments, and the interactions between them. From morphology and kinematics to spatial relations, affordances, and manipulation, geometric representations can connect perception, reasoning, and action.

This workshop brings together researchers exploring geometry-aware approaches to physical intelligence and human-centric robotics. We will examine how geometric structure can support cross-embodiment transfer, spatial understanding, robot learning, contact-rich manipulation, and safe physical interaction.

/ Detailed themes

Discussion areas

Topics that connect geometry, embodiment, perception, learning, and physical interaction.

  • Geometry of robot embodiment, morphology, and kinematic structure
  • Configuration spaces, movement representations, and cross-embodiment skill transfer
  • Geometry-aware 3D spatial understanding and scene representation
  • Object–environment geometry, spatial relations, affordances, and actionable representations
  • 3D/4D geometric world models for physical reasoning and prediction
  • Geometry-aware Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and World Action Models (WAMs)
  • Geometric action representations and generative policies for robot learning
  • Geometry-aware manipulation and contact-rich physical interaction
  • Geometric priors, equivariance, and structured representations for generalizable robot learning
  • Geometric constraints for safe physical human-robot interaction
